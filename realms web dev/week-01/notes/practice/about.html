About BrightPath

Skills Summit

BrightPath Skill Summit is a hands-on learning event designed to help young people gain in-demand skills, connect with industry experts, and take the next step in their career journey and personal development.

Our Mission

This summit is for:

  • Students and recent graduates.
  • Young Professionals.
  • Job Seekers and Career Changers.
  • Any individual interested in building digital skills.

Our Vision

To empower young people with the skills, knowledge, and connections they need to succeed in the digital economy.

We envision a future where every young person has access to high-quality, practical learning experiences that prepare them for the jobs of tomorrow.

Our GOal

To bridge the gap between education and industry by providing practical, real-world learning experiences. Mentorship and connections in the tech and digital space.

Key Benefits

  • Practical skill for real-world application.
  • Access to industry experts and mentors.
  • Networking with peers and professionals.
  • Certification of participation.

A full day of learning, networking and growth.
