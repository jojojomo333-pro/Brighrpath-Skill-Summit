Frequently Asked Questions

What is the BrightPath Skills Summit?

A practical learning event where young people build skills, meet industry experts, and explore new career opportunities.

Who can attend the event?

Students, recent graduates, young professionals, job seekers, career changers, and anyone interested in digital skills are welcome.

How do I register?

Complete the registration form on our Register page to reserve your spot.

Is there a refund policy?

For help with a registration or refund request, contact us by email and include your registration details.

Will there be a certificate?

Yes. Participants will receive a certificate of participation.
